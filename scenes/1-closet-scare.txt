INT. CHILD'S BEDROOM - NIGHT. A small bedroom, about 5 by 4 metres. A bed against the left wall, a tall wardrobe closet against the back wall, slightly right of centre, its door ajar. MAYA (30s) stands in the middle of the room holding a flashlight, listening. A faint knocking comes from inside the closet. She edges toward it. The knocking stops. Silence. She reaches for the handle — and the closet door bangs open on its own. We want a slow creeping push toward Maya and the closet, then a violent whip to the closet door on the bang.
