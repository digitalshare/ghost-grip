INT. BATHROOM - NIGHT. A cramped bathroom set, 4 by 3.5 metres. A sink and mirror cabinet on the back wall, a bathtub along the right wall with its curtain drawn. NORA (40s) washes her face at the sink, eyes closed. She looks up into the mirror: nothing. She opens the cabinet, closes it — and in the mirror a pale woman now stands behind her, by the bathtub. Nora spins around. Nobody there. Slow Dutch drift during the washing, a mirror reveal, and a fast turn-around reveal of the empty bathtub.
