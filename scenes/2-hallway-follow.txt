INT. HOTEL CORRIDOR SET - NIGHT. A long corridor flat built on stage, 8 metres wide and 4 deep, with three doors along the back wall. LEO (12) walks slowly left to right along the corridor, trailing his fingers on the wall. At the third door he stops: two figures in identical dresses stand at the far end, by the right wall. He freezes. The camera should follow him low and steady from behind his shoulder, then hold as the figures are revealed.
